The cleaned subway dataset has 1868 rows and 19 columns. Each row
describes a subway entrance or exit. The retained variables describe the
line, station name, station latitude and longitude, routes served, entry
permission, vending availability, entrance type, and ADA compliance. I
cleaned the column names, retained only the requested variables, and
converted entry permission from YES/NO to TRUE/FALSE. I imported all
route columns as character variables because train names may contain
letters or numbers.

### Count distinct stations and examine entry permission

``` r
station_df =
  subway_df |>
  distinct(line, station_name)

ada_station_df =
  subway_df |>
  filter(ada == TRUE) |>
  distinct(line, station_name)

no_vending_df =
  subway_df |>
  filter(vending == "NO")
```

There are 465 distinct stations, identified by the combination of line
and station name. Of these, 84 are ADA compliant. Counting distinct
stations avoids counting a station repeatedly because it has multiple
entrances.

Among the 183 entrances/exits without vending, 69 allow entry. The
proportion allowing entry is 0.377, or 37.7%. For a logical variable,
the mean equals the proportion of TRUE values.
